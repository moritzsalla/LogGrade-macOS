Map screenshots — not committed
===============================

docs/PIPELINE.md, "A theory this disproved", cites two independent views of the same
corner to establish what colour the building actually is. Both were screenshots of
third-party map services and are NOT in this repo.

Why not
-------
Google Maps and Apple Maps both restrict redistribution of their imagery, and the
Street View capture carried a "© 2026 Google" watermark. This is the same constraint
luts/apple/SOURCE.txt reasons about for Apple's LUTs: free to look at, not free to
republish. A public repo redistributing them is the part that is not allowed.

The measurement in PIPELINE.md stands on its own; the screenshots were corroboration,
not evidence anyone needs to re-derive.

How to reproduce them
---------------------
The corner is Den Brielstraat, Amsterdam, at 52.3851203, 4.8586739.

  Google Street View   https://www.google.com/maps/@52.3851203,4.8586739,3a,34.3y,216.57h,98.92t
                       Overcast capture. Shows the brick frontage under flat light.

  Apple Maps           Search the same coordinates, Look Around, face the same heading.
                       Sunny capture, which is the point: two lighting conditions agreeing
                       on the brick colour is what made the reading independent of weather.

Both were captured in September 2026. Street View re-shoots periodically, so a later
capture may differ; "See more dates" gives the archive.
