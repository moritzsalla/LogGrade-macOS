Where they came from
--------------------
GitHub, downloaded 11 Sep 2026 — free, public, no login:

  https://github.com/YahiaAngelo/Film-Luts

Raw file pattern:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YahiaAngelo/Film-Luts/main/luts/<subdir>/<name>.cube

The shipped one:
  luts/negative_old/kodak_portra_400_nc.cube
  TITLE "kodak_portra_400_nc", LUT_3D_SIZE 13

Local filenames are prefixed with their upstream subdirectory (negative_new_,
negative_old_, negative_color_) because the same stock appears in several
renderings. The suffixes are upstream's contrast variants: - / + / ++ ; "nc" =
neutral contrast, "vc" = vivid, "uc" = ultra, "hc" = high contrast.

Ultimately generated by G'MIC's film-emulation dataset (gmic.eu), built from real
film response data.

Known limitations — read before swapping these around
-----------------------------------------------------
1. They are all 13x13x13. That is coarse next to Apple's 65^3 CST. Tetrahedral
   interpolation (interp=tetrahedral) smooths it; use nothing weaker.
2. They emulate a photographic PRINT of an already-developed image, not a cinema
   tone response applied to scene-linear light. They supply colour character and
   almost no contrast — which is exactly why this pipeline needs a separate tone
   stage (luts/tone/shipped.cube). Don't expect a film look from the LUT alone.
3. Every free Portra LUT reachable without a gated download turned out to be the
   same 13^3 G'MIC grid, so "find a better one" was a dead end. A genuinely
   better look would be a print-film emulation (Kodak 2383-class), which expects
   log or Cineon input rather than Rec.709 — a different pipeline shape.

Searched and rejected: cinecolor.io, freepresets.com, presetpro.com (all gated
behind email signup or paid), DocM88/Free-Lut-Pack (.xmp Lightroom presets only,
not .cube).

Licence — MIT, and these ARE committed
--------------------------------------
YahiaAngelo/Film-Luts is MIT licensed, which permits redistribution provided the
copyright notice travels with it. The upstream notice is therefore kept beside
the .cube files as LICENSE_FILM_LUTS.txt (Copyright (c) 2024 Yahia).

This is the opposite of luts/apple/, which is NOT redistributable and is
gitignored. If you add another look LUT, check its terms before committing it —
most "free" LUT packs are free-to-use, not free-to-redistribute.
