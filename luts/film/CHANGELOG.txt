Film looks — provenance and changes
===================================

These cubes encode spektrafilm profiles and are therefore under CC BY-SA 4.0
(SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt, beside this file, unmodified as it asks).

  Film modelling: spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato
  Canonical source: https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm
  License: CC BY-SA 4.0
  Modified by Moritz Salla, 2026-09-15 (spektrafilm main, v0.3.4) — see below.

Every cube is Apple Log (full-range RGB, as ffmpeg lut3d reads it) in, Rec.709
display code values for Apple playback (scripts/cubefile.py) out, 65^3, and replaces Apple's
AppleLogToRec709 cube as the conversion. Each TITLE carries its exact bake
parameters. They are baked for BT.1886 (gamma 2.4) and re-encoded to Apple
playback (gamma 502/256), value by value (the same light, a different code
value); "display=apple1.961" in each TITLE marks it. On 2026-09-21
super8_kodachrome64 was rebaked from its TITLE (reproducing the previous cube
exactly) and re-encoded from "display=apple", the inverse BT.709 OETF, whose
shadows played darker. portra160 and the portra800 derived from it carry the
partner's edit, whose codes are Adobe RGB, so they are re-encoded as Adobe RGB
light instead ("from=adobergb-power": code^(563/502), primaries left alone;
converting them clipped 8% of the lattice).

What was modified
-----------------
portra160.cube, portra800.cube
  spektrafilm produces the developed negative's CMY dye densities only (its
  own scan of a negative is not inverted). The positive is NOT spektrafilm's:
  a lab-scan model written here inverts each channel's density, balances it
  onto the green layer's grey-scale curve, then puts back the stock's own layer
  crossover as a linear scanner anchored at -5 stops and mid grey would keep it
  ("crossover", capped at half a stop). It renders through a Michaelis-Menten
  scanner tone curve anchored at mid grey, then applies Oklab chroma, hue
  curves (hue-vs-hue, hue-vs-chroma, hue-vs-lightness, periodic splines
  through 12 knots, fitted from the window parameters in the TITLE),
  subtractive density, a gamut fit that reduces chroma at constant lightness
  and hue instead of clipping, and a display black lift. DIR coupler amount
  raised above the profile default (the "dir" value in each TITLE).
    portra160       kodak_portra_160, +1 stop, scanned
    portra800       kodak_portra_800, +1 stop, scanned

portra160.cube, 2026-10-02: the partner's edit on the stock
  The cube above, then the partner's edit of their Portra 160 lab scans as
  three per-channel curves, measured scan -> edit on the same pixels of two
  frames (bin means, half the average slope at least, unit slope above scan
  code 0.9 where the scans hold no pixels), then Oklab chroma x0.62. Chroma and
  the preset's density (match.reference_stops -1.0) are the two numbers fitted
  to the edits, registered to Apple Log video of the same scenes; both apply to
  every colour alike. The curves and chroma are on the "#" lines, hash in the
  TITLE ("fit=partner-edit:"). Median error against the edits 0.024 / 0.018
  Oklab. It replaces a 2026-09-21 fit to the same pairs (0.0073) that matched
  the facade by overfitting it and turned skin grey and reds magenta.

portra800.cube, 2026-10-02: derived from portra160.cube
  Portra 800 is the 160 look at a higher speed, not a separate fit: Oklab
  lightness contrast 0.9 about 0.6, black lifted 0.02 (fading to 0 at white),
  chroma 0.9 ("800=derived" in the TITLE, the recipe on a "#" line). Its
  coarser grain is the preset's.

super8_kodachrome64.cube
  kodak_kodachrome_64 reversal, +0.2 stop, scanned directly (io.scan_film:
  the kodak_2383 print named in the TITLE is not applied), scanner white and
  black correction re-enabled after spektrafilm's LUT mode resets them. Then
  an Oklab trim: lightness S-curve (contrast 1.15 about 0.55), chroma x1.2
  faded out below lightness 0.2-0.45 so the warm toe keeps its blue channel,
  a cast toward blue (Oklab b -0.018, rising from neutral black) that takes
  out the stock's warm cast, reds turned 8 degrees toward scarlet, sky blue
  chroma x1.8, cyan-to-sky turned 35 degrees toward deep blue, foliage chroma
  x0.7, and the same gamut fit. Chroma, sky, skin and red were matched to
  RawTherapee's Kodachrome 64 Hald CLUT (measured, not baked from).

Not reproducible from this repo alone: the bake scripts import spektrafilm
(GPLv3) and are kept out of it. The TITLE lines record every parameter.
