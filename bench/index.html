    Curve maths is a port of make-tone-lut.py. The luma-only application mirrors
    mergeplanes=0x001112: all three channels shift by the luma delta, chroma untouched —
    which is what stops a contrast curve turning saturated signage neon. Sampler targets are RAL
    specs (1021 plate yellow, 3020 traffic red, 5017 traffic blue); objects in shade read darker and
    less saturated than spec, so read them as hue and ratio references, not absolute exposure.
